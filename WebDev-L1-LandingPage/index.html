COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you get started.

A few practical questions for anyone beginning their technology career.

Do I need previous experience?

The sample paths are designed for beginners. Basic computer skills and consistent practice are useful starting points.

Will I build projects?

The concept curriculum emphasizes practice through projects that learners can document and add to a portfolio.

Will this guarantee me a job?

No. Training and career preparation can help you develop and present your skills, but employment depends on many factors.

How do I choose a learning path?

Consider the type of work you enjoy, review the skills listed in each path, and start with the fundamentals.
